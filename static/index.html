The street already knows what it likes

Chains pay for audience research. The café on the corner guesses. Footfall does the research from the places your neighbours already go to.

  1. Find the block

    Qloo lists the places within a walk of your shop that locals actually go to: the bar two doors down, the cinema round the corner.

  2. Carry its taste across

    Those places become the signal for music, books and brands: what do the people who go there listen to, read and buy? Then compare with the wider area.

  3. Find the taste gap

    What the street likes that your shop doesn't show yet, one domain at a time: music genres, dishes, book genres.

  4. Dig, write, check

    Nemotron makes its own Qloo calls, then drafts the plan. A checker deletes anything Qloo didn't return.
